A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

THE
LOWDOWN.

ARE YOU REALLY A STUDENT-LED TEAM? +

Yes. We are students taking on freelance creative and printing projects. We work carefully to produce professional-quality results and will confirm what we can realistically deliver before accepting an order.

CAN I BOOK A PHOTO BOOTH? +

Tell us the event date, location, expected guests and the keepsakes you would like. We will confirm whether a photo booth setup and photo keychain or fridge magnet services are available for your event.

CAN I ORDER DIY KITS FOR A BIRTHDAY? +

Yes, request the age group, number of children, party theme and date. We can discuss suitable craft activities, kit contents, pricing and adult supervision requirements.

CAN I PRINT FAMOUS HEROES OR MEMES? +

We love comics and memes. We offer original comic-style art and can discuss artwork you own or have permission to reproduce; recognizable characters, logos and third-party images may need licensing.

CAN I ORDER JUST ONE ITEM? +

Send us the product you have in mind. Minimum order requirements can vary by product, material, and production method, and we'll confirm what's possible.

CAN I SEND MY OWN DESIGN OR LOGO? +

Yes — start the order on WhatsApp, then share your artwork or logo files directly in the chat. We can discuss the best print or finishing option.

HOW MUCH DOES IT COST? +

Because these are personalized products, pricing depends on the item, quantity, material, finish and design. Send your cart to request an accurate quote.

DO YOU DO BULK / CORPORATE ORDERS? +

We welcome business enquiries. Use the business brief above for apparel, branded packaging, merchandise, events and more.

WHAT ABOUT DELIVERY AND PRODUCTION TIMES? +

Availability, delivery options and turnaround depend on the product and order details. Let us know your destination and required date when you enquire.
